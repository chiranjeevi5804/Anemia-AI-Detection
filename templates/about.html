THE PROJECT

About Anemia AI

A research prototype exploring computer vision and image-based detection of nail and tongue appearance.

◈

Overview

Anemia AI uses an existing trained YOLOv8 object-detection model to identify patterns in photographs of fingernails and tongues. The system produces bounding boxes, class labels and confidence scores.

✧

System Approach

Images are uploaded through Flask, decoded with OpenCV, processed by YOLOv8, and returned with model annotations. This is a demonstration of image classification, not a clinical test.

WORKFLOW

Detection Process

1

Image Upload

Choose a clear JPG, PNG, BMP or WEBP photograph of a fingernail or tongue.

2

Preprocessing

The image is decoded and passed to the model's built-in inference pipeline.

3

Object Detection

Predicted regions receive class labels and confidence scores.

4

View the annotated image and the model's detected classes.
